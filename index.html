Muscle Growth Principles

Stretching Under Load

Performing exercises through full range of motion is crucial for hypertrophy. When a muscle is fully stretched under load, greater microdamage and activation of anabolic pathways occur.

Source: Jeff Nippard's YouTube Channel

Intensity: RIR 1-3

"Reps in Reserve" (RIR) is the number of repetitions you could still perform. Train with RIR 1-3 for maximum growth stimulation.

🌾 Carbohydrates and Glycemic Index

📚 What is Glycemic Index (GI)?

GI is a 0-100 scale showing how quickly carbohydrates from a given product raise blood glucose levels. Low GI products cause a slower, more stable sugar rise, which is beneficial for:

  • • Maintaining stable energy
  • • Appetite and satiety control
  • • Better blood sugar control
  • • Reducing sudden insulin spikes
🟢 Low GI (<55):
  • Leafy vegetables: GI <15 (spinach, lettuce, arugula)
  • Broccoli, cauliflower: GI ~10
  • Berries (blueberries, raspberries): GI ~25
  • Apples: GI ~36
  • Oranges: GI ~45
  • Lentils: GI ~29
  • Chickpeas: GI ~33
  • Beans: GI ~24
  • Quinoa: GI ~53
  • Pears: GI ~33
  • Natural yogurt: GI ~14
🟡 Medium GI (56-69):
  • Oatmeal: GI ~55
  • Brown rice: GI ~50-55
  • Sweet potatoes: GI ~63
  • Bananas (ripe): GI ~62
  • Whole grain bread: GI ~56
  • Whole grain pasta: GI ~58
  • Buckwheat: GI ~54
🔴 High GI (>70) - Limit:
  • White rice: GI ~73
  • Potatoes (boiled): GI ~78
  • White bread: GI ~75
  • Watermelon: GI ~72
  • Corn flakes: GI ~81
  • Glucose: GI 100 (reference point)

⚡ Tip: Combining carbohydrates with protein and fats slows absorption and lowers the effective GI of a meal. Fiber also slows digestion.

03 — Suplementy

Supplements

⚡ Creatine

One of the most well-researched supplements. Helps muscles produce energy during intense effort.

  • ✅ Increased strength and power
  • ✅ Muscle mass growth
  • ✅ Improved endurance
  • ✅ Cognitive benefits

Dosage: 3-5g daily

🥤 Protein Powder

Convenient way to supplement daily protein needs.

  • 🥛 Whey: Fast absorbing
  • Casein: Slow absorbing
  • Soy: Complete plant-based
  • 🌿 Pea/Rice: For vegans

When: After workout or as a supplement
